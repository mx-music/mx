# Erbarme dich, mein Gott

`bwv244.39.mxl` is Bach's aria "Erbarme dich, mein Gott" from the St Matthew Passion, BWV 244/39, for violin, alto and organ, as encoded by MuseScore user 30010606 at https://musescore.com/user/30010606/scores/5331148.
Bach's music is in the public domain, and the encoding is under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
`aria.exs` is what `mix mx.import` writes from it, unchanged otherwise, so it is under the same license.
`dub.exs` arranges the aria as a dub, taking its harmony, its bass and the violin's and alto's melodies from the encoding, and is under the same license.
`score.exs` holds the notes `piano.exs` and `paraphrase.exs` take from the encoding, and is under the same license.
`piano.exs` arranges the aria for the piano alone, and is under the same license.
`paraphrase.exs` reworks the aria freely for the piano, with a prelude, a cadenza and a coda written for it, and is under the same license.
