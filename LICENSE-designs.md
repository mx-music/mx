# Synth designs

## mx's own designs

mx's synths and effects are its own, under mx's MIT License in the LICENSE file at the root of the repository, and are played by mxs, mx's engine.
The guitar is measured from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios' guitar recordings, https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISguitar.html, which may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions; no audio from them is part of mx.
The piano is measured from the Steinway B grand recordings, with and without the sustain pedal, of Versilian Studios' Versilian Community Sample Library, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, under CC0; no audio from them is part of mx.
The harp is measured from the folk harp recordings of Versilian Studios' Versilian Community Sample Library, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, under CC0; no audio from them is part of mx.
The glockenspiel is measured from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios' orchestral bells recordings, https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISbells.html, which may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions, and compared with the glockenspiel recordings of Versilian Studios' VSCO 2 Community Edition, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, under CC0; no audio from them is part of mx.
The xylophone is measured from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios' xylophone recordings, https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISxylophone.html, which may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions, and compared with the xylophone recordings of Versilian Studios' VSCO 2 Community Edition, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, under CC0; no audio from them is part of mx.

The clave is measured from the claves recordings of Versilian Studios' Versilian Community Sample Library, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, and VSCO 2 Community Edition, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, under CC0, and was compared with the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios' claves recordings, https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIShandpercussion.html, which may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions; no audio from them is part of mx.

## Airwindows

Two of mx's effects follow effects from Airwindows, Chris Johnson's collection of plugins, https://github.com/airwindows/airwindows: bitcrusher follows DeRez, and chorus follows Chorus.
mx's designs are written anew and sound close to those effects rather than identical to them.
